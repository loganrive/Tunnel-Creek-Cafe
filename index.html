Our Story

A Local Gem at the Quiet Edge of Tahoe

Tunnel Creek Cafe is your perfect pit stop before — or after — hitting the trail. We serve up fresh breakfasts, hearty lunches, and cold drinks in a laid-back atmosphere surrounded by the natural beauty of Lake Tahoe.

Whether you're fueling up for a hike or winding down after one, we've got the food, the drinks, and the views to make it memorable.
